ATELIER — THE ART OF SKILL EXCHANGE
====================================

Atelier is a platform that brings people together to exchange skills and knowledge.
Think of it as a living gallery where every skill is treated as a craft, and every
exchange is a collaboration between two people.


WHAT IT DOES
------------

People sign up, create a profile, and list two kinds of skills:

  1. Skills they can TEACH — things they know and are willing to share with others.
  2. Skills they want to LEARN — things they are curious about and want someone
     to teach them.

Once your skills are listed, you can browse the "Discover" page to see what others
in the community are teaching or seeking. When you find a skill that interests you,
you propose an exchange — a swap where you offer to teach something in return for
learning what they know.

The other person receives your proposal and can accept or decline it. Once an
exchange is accepted and completed, both sides leave a review for each other,
building trust and reputation over time.


THE FOUR MAIN AREAS
-------------------

  STUDIO (Dashboard)
    Your personal home base. Shows your stats (how many skills you're teaching,
    seeking, how many exchanges you have, how many are pending), your listed
    skills, and your most recent exchange activity.

  DISCOVER
    The gallery. Browse every skill listed by other members. Filter by category,
    search by keyword, and filter to see only "teaching" or "seeking" listings.
    Click "Propose exchange" on any skill to send a swap request to that person.

  EXCHANGES
    Your inbox for skill swaps. See all your exchanges — incoming requests others
    sent to you, outgoing requests you sent, and everything in between. Accept,
    decline, or mark exchanges as completed. Leave a star rating and review after
    a completed swap.

  PROFILE
    Your public page. Edit your name, bio, location, and avatar color. See your
    teaching and learning skills side by side, add or remove skills, and view
    your average rating from reviews.


HOW AN EXCHANGE WORKS
---------------------

  Step 1:  You find a skill on the Discover page that you want to learn.
  Step 2:  You click "Propose exchange" and write a message introducing yourself
           and what you can offer in return.
  Step 3:  The other person sees your proposal in their Exchanges inbox. They
           accept or decline it.
  Step 4:  If accepted, you arrange how to teach each other (that part is up to
           you — Atelier handles the connection, not the lesson itself).
  Step 5:  When you're done, either person marks the exchange as completed.
  Step 6:  Both sides leave a review with a 1-to-5 star rating and a comment.


THE DESIGN PHILOSOPHY
---------------------

Atelier is built around the metaphor of an art studio or gallery. The color
palette draws from natural, earthy tones — saffron, terracotta, sage, teal, plum,
and warm ink — rather than the typical tech-startup blues and purples. The
typography pairs Fraunces (a refined serif) with Inter (a clean sans-serif) to
create a sense of craft and intentionality.

Animations are used throughout to make the experience feel alive: parallax blobs
that respond to mouse movement on the hero, magnetic buttons that pull toward
your cursor, scroll-triggered reveals that fade content in as you read, shimmering
gradient text, and subtle floating elements. The goal is for the site to feel
less like a tool and more like a place you want to spend time in.


THE TECHNOLOGY
--------------

  Frontend:    React + TypeScript + Vite
  Styling:     Tailwind CSS with a custom art-inspired color system
  Icons:       Lucide React
  Backend:     Supabase (database, authentication, row-level security)
  Database:    PostgreSQL with four tables — profiles, skills, exchanges,
               and reviews — all protected by row-level security policies so
               users can only modify their own data.

Authentication uses Supabase's email and password system. Every user gets a
profile automatically when they sign up. All data access is secured with
row-level security policies enforced at the database level.


SUMMARY
-------

Atelier is a skill-exchange platform. You teach what you know, learn what you
don't, and connect with people who share your passions — all in a space designed
to feel like a gallery rather than a marketplace.
